The Pedagogical Gap

Static Courseware Leaves Students Guessing and Faculty in the Dark

The Generic AI Hazard

Uncalibrated AI Leads to Confusion

When students turn to open-web chatbots, they receive hallucinated, out-of-scope answers that pull them down irrelevant rabbit holes. Generic tools cannot follow a lecturer’s specific syllabus, leading students to memorize content that won’t appear on exams.

The Assessment Bottleneck

Limited Past Papers Cause Rote Memorization

Faculty don’t have the time to hand-write dozens of new practice papers each semester. With limited practice materials available, students end up rote-learning past question phrasing rather than developing true conceptual understanding.

The Midterm Blind Spot

Zero Visibility into Where Cohorts Are Struggling

Between the first lecture and the midterm exam, lecturers have no systematic way to see where students are hitting friction. Interventions often happen too late, after grades have already dropped.
